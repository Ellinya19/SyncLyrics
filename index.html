HOW TO USE
Use Toxi's TTML Renderer to view your TTML files.
\ Insert \ between characters to split a word into multiple sync units while keeping the original display.
Example: Do\ra\e\mon → synced as Do · ra · e · mon, displayed as Doraemon.
... Entering ... (ellipsis) in the lyrics will represent a section without lyrics. When exported, the ellipsis will be split into three separate dots spaced evenly apart.
